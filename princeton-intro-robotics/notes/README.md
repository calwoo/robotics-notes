# Lecture Notes

## Available notes

### [Lecture 1 — Introduction to Robotics](lecture-01-intro-to-robotics.md)

Definitions and history of robotics; the sense-think-act abstraction; planning, estimation, and learning; sources of uncertainty; course structure and Crazyflie hardware.

Primary materials: [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cMhFAq53v1c), [slides](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/oltefsy9ihq4efgxvtyn9/Lecture1.pdf?dl=0&rlkey=4kc7a1r2pja5z932phyakcq73).

### [Lecture 2 — Discrete Motion Planning](lecture-02-discrete-motion-planning.md)

Configuration space, collision-free planning, grid discretization, graph search, breadth-first search, depth-first search, Dijkstra's algorithm, and A*.

Primary materials: [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aVmqaI2WmBU), [slides](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/pnwucnirlwgviqwaj67hl/Lecture2.pdf?dl=0&rlkey=6s2et2apw9rtnvr8x2ke243sp).

### [Lecture 3 — Randomized Planning with RRTs](lecture-03-randomized-planning-rrts.md)

Continuous-space planning with rapidly-exploring randomized trees: configuration-space sampling, nearest-neighbor extension, collision checking, step size, parent-pointer path recovery, and RRT variants.

Primary materials: [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KDIe1094FMo), [slides](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/5uv2vt76nqj4b32vr4i37/Lecture3.pdf?dl=0&rlkey=nx8jjqjlvmgcqqhb8r1p6okzp).

### [Lecture 4 — Quadrotor Dynamics](lecture-04-quadrotor-dynamics.md)

Dynamic feasibility, planar and 3D quadrotor models, state-space form, thrust and moment inputs, motor models, Euler angles, and the connection from geometric paths to executable trajectories.

Primary materials: [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=upI4KaGawyE), [slides](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/v8bp2gyhpixill7c70u7q/Lecture4_slides.pdf?dl=0&rlkey=h44i0kp1bpmxk3zdg65gvqu29), [notes](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/onuvrtpb64sytgp78towu/Lecture4_notes.pdf?dl=0&rlkey=yo9dpy9jxt7730f0ex3sjdcuw).

Local copy: [Lecture 4 notes PDF](Lecture4_notes.pdf).
